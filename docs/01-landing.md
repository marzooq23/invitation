# 01 — LANDING

## Purpose

The landing section is the first impression of the invitation.

It should immediately communicate:

- This is a wedding invitation
- The names of the couple
- The wedding date
- A warm invitation to the guest

The section should be visually focused and should not contain too much information.

## Bismillah

Display an appropriate Bismillah.

English-Arabic Text:
```text
Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem
```

Arabic Text:
```text
بِسْمِ ٱللهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيْمِ
```
 
English Meaning: 
```text
In the Name of Allah, the Most Beneficent, the Most Merciful
```  

## Wedding Introduction

Example:

```text
With the blessings of Allah (SWT)
and the love of our families,

[Groom] Mohamed Marzooq

&

[Bride] Farwizah

we invite you to celebrate the beginning of their new journey together.
```

## Wedding Date

Display the primary wedding date prominently.

```text
[DAY] 20
[MONTH] 12
[YEAR] 2026
```

Date: `20 DECEMBER 2026`

## Countdown

A countdown may be included if the wedding is in the future.

Possible countdown values:

`[ DAYS ]   [ HOURS ]   [ MINUTES ]   [ SECONDS ]`

The countdown should be optional and should disappear gracefully after the wedding date has passed.

## Opening CTA

A subtle invitation to continue exploring.

```text
EXPLORE THE INVITATION
↓
```

## Recommended Landing Information

The landing page should answer only:

- Who is getting married?
- When is it happening?
